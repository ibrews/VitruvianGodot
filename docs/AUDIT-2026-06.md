# Visual-Fidelity Audit — June 2026 (v1.0, HEAD ~5b7d672)

**Question asked:** is this project worth continuing next to MetaHuman, and what would make
the demo *impressive*? This is a cold, evidence-based read — every claim below comes from a
fresh capture (all frames in [docs/audit-2026-06/](audit-2026-06/), captured 2026-06-09 from
the live project via the FACE_FORCE / HAIR_SHOT / FRAME_BODY / CINE_PREVIEW hooks at 900×900,
Vulkan Forward+). No fixes were implemented as part of this audit.

---

## 1. The cold read against the MetaHuman bar

**TL;DR: at full-body distance this character holds up surprisingly well. The moment the
camera gets within a meter of the face, four things break the illusion in roughly the first
second: the hair part-line, the eyes, the skin speckle, and the neck.** MetaHuman survives a
frame-filling close-up; this asset currently does not. That gap is concentrated and most of
it is addressable — none of the top issues below is "Godot can't do it", they are content and
shading bugs.

What already *works* (credit where due, this matters for the verdict):

- **Silhouette and proportions in profile** ([head_profile_hair.png](audit-2026-06/head_profile_hair.png))
  — the face profile is genuinely good. Nose, lips, chin line read as a real person.
- **The FACS mouth tech works** — `Mouth_Large_Opened` opens a real mouth with real teeth and
  tongue ([face_jawopen.png](audit-2026-06/face_jawopen.png)). The *shading* of the interior is
  the problem (see #4), not the deformation.
- **Mixamo locomotion + spring-bone hair physics** — the walk reads naturally at body distance
  ([body_walk.png](audit-2026-06/body_walk.png)); hair lags and settles plausibly in Turn.
- **The look-dev tool itself** (50 sliders, lighting presets, anim/expression buttons) is a real
  asset for the pitch — MetaHuman doesn't give you a hackable, redistributable tuner.

### Ranked: the top 5 "this looks amateur" issues

#### #1 — Hair: the part-line scalp gap and groom inconsistency (the single worst tell)

Evidence: [head_front_hair.png](audit-2026-06/head_front_hair.png),
[cine_closeup_29.png](audit-2026-06/cine_closeup_29.png),
[head_profile_hair.png](audit-2026-06/head_profile_hair.png),
[head_back_hair.png](audit-2026-06/head_back_hair.png)

- **A bright bald wedge at the front part-line.** In every front/¾ shot there is a pale
  triangle of bare scalp where the part opens up — it reads instantly as "wig glued on wrong"
  or thinning hair. This is the first thing any viewer's eye lands on. The `lift_front()`
  forehead push widened the part; the scalp cap under it is skin-bright instead of
  shadow-dark, so the gap glows.
- **Color is not stable across angles.** Front-lit the hair is dark brunette; in the profile
  shot the whole side mass goes *golden straw* (anisotropic highlight + backlight tint +
  hair-kicker at 3.2 are hue-shifting it). It looks like two different wigs in one orbit.
- **The crown layers like lasagna.** In the cinematic close-up the card layers stack in
  visible horizontal shelves; strand clumps at the back read rope-/dreadlock-like
  ([head_back_hair.png](audit-2026-06/head_back_hair.png)), with blue specular sparkle
  artifacts (specular aliasing) glittering in the dark mass.

MetaHuman comparison: hair is *the* known hardest part; even MH card LODs look better than
this mainly because their groom density/flow maps are authored, the scalp never shows, and
highlight color is constrained. Most of our gap here is fixable at the texture/shader/groom
level without new tech.

#### #2 — Eyes: dead, decorated, and (in the hero shot) closed

Evidence: [z_eyes_tuned.png](audit-2026-06/z_eyes_tuned.png),
[z_eye_macro.png](audit-2026-06/z_eye_macro.png),
[cine_closeup_29.png](audit-2026-06/cine_closeup_29.png),
[body_turn.png](audit-2026-06/body_turn.png)

- **The shipped tuned look has a magenta/pink band on both upper lids** (the eyeshadow slider
  is baked on in `look_settings.json`). At close range it reads as a shading glitch, not makeup.
- **Gaze is fixed slightly down and inward** in the tuned look — the classic "doll stare past
  you" that triggers the uncanny response immediately.
- **No lid-contact anatomy.** The eye macro shows it clearly: no lacrimal caruncle in the inner
  corner, no wet tear-line, no ambient-occlusion ring where the lid meets the eyeball, and a
  faint rectangular card edge visible at the eye corner. The eyeball "floats" in the socket.
- **At body distance the eyes blow out to blank white dots** ([body_turn.png](audit-2026-06/body_turn.png))
  — mannequin face — because the sclera is bright and the iris is small/dark with no
  mid-distance read.
- **The cinematic hero close-up (~29.5s) catches the character mid-blink/eyes-closed**
  ([cine_closeup_29.png](audit-2026-06/cine_closeup_29.png)) — the one beat designed to sell
  the face, and she has her eyes shut in it on this preview frame.

The bitter part: the CharMorph data ships *real* eye geometry (UDIM tile 1005 + 4K
`sclera_col/disp/rough` EXRs + `iris_*` EXRs), `Look_*` gaze morphs, **`Tearline.blend` and
`Lacrimal_Caruncle.blend` as ready-made assets** — all unused. We hand-rolled a procedural
eyeball instead.

#### #3 — Skin: waxy + glitter speckle, and the neck is still two-tone under warm light

Evidence: [z_eyes_tuned.png](audit-2026-06/z_eyes_tuned.png) (speckle across nose bridge),
[z_neck_glow.png](audit-2026-06/z_neck_glow.png), [eyes_closeup.png](audit-2026-06/eyes_closeup.png)

- **White speckle dots** scatter across the nose bridge, forehead and cheeks in every tuned
  close-up — SSS noise / specular aliasing reading as glitter or sweat beads. On the forehead
  there are also smeary vertical specular streaks (the "greasy" look).
- **No mid-frequency detail.** The face albedo is a smooth tone-blend; there are no pores,
  no cavity darkening, no roughness breakup. At portrait distance the skin reads as vinyl.
  Meanwhile **~1 GB of unused 4K skin EXRs** (`skin_light_col`/`skin_dark_col` albedos,
  `skin_cavity_rough_coat`, `skin_disp`, `age_skin_disp` — tiles 1001-1004 covering face AND
  body) sit in the CharMorph data.
- **The neck still splits from the face.** [z_neck_glow.png](audit-2026-06/z_neck_glow.png):
  under the warm catch-light the neck column glows salmon-pink while the jaw above is darker
  and redder — a visible boundary at the jawline. Root cause is the eye catch-light OmniLight
  (energy 1.6, parked ~0.5 m in front of the face) flooding the neck and chin from below,
  *plus* the body neck having no albedo texture (flat tone). The known **dark collar notch**
  is also visible at the right collar.

#### #4 — The open mouth reads "muppet"

Evidence: [z_mouth_jaw.png](audit-2026-06/z_mouth_jaw.png),
[face_surprise.png](audit-2026-06/face_surprise.png)

The deformation is right (jaw, teeth, tongue all move) but the shading kills it: the mouth
interior is lit as brightly as the face, the tongue is a large flat-pink slab filling the
whole opening, and the teeth ring around the aperture like dentures. A real mouth is mostly
*darkness* with teeth catching light at the top. Also: "surprise" bares clenched-looking
teeth ([face_surprise.png](audit-2026-06/face_surprise.png)) — it reads grimace, not surprise —
and "sad" is nearly indistinguishable from neutral ([face_sad.png](audit-2026-06/face_sad.png) vs
[face_neutral.png](audit-2026-06/face_neutral.png)). The mouth ships its own unused 4K
`mouth_col/disp/rough` EXRs (tile 1006); we use a 1K PNG.

#### #5 — Wardrobe, staging, and stillness undercut everything

Evidence: [face_neutral.png](audit-2026-06/face_neutral.png) (shoulder spikes),
[body_full.png](audit-2026-06/body_full.png), [cine_mid_10.png](audit-2026-06/cine_mid_10.png)

- **The inflated shirt has pointy shoulder spikes** — the +6 mm inflation (the neck fix) turned
  the shoulder seams into sharp peaks visible in *every* upper-body shot, and the torso
  balloons (reads "pregnant in a sail"). Pants are an untextured grey tube; the character is
  **barefoot**, which reads asset-store, not art direction.
- **Staging is an asset viewer, not a film.** [cine_mid_10.png](audit-2026-06/cine_mid_10.png):
  flat grey floor plane, visible horizon line, even frontal light, dead-center framing. No
  vignette, no grade, no depth-of-field falloff doing work, no background interest. This is
  the cheapest place to buy "impressive" and it's currently spending nothing.
- **The character is too still between anims.** No breathing, no weight shift, no head
  micro-motion, symmetric face at idle. Stillness is what flips "stylized person" into
  "mannequin" — and it's free to fix on a rig we already drive procedurally.

## 3. Prioritized fix plan

### Tier 1 — low-hanging fruit (each ≤ ~half a day; together ≈ 2-3 days, transforms the demo)

| # | Fix | Effort | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Close/darken the hair part-line** | 2-4 h | Darken the scalp cap to near-hair albedo under the part; reduce `lift_front()` push and add a row of fine "baby hair" cards along the parting in `build_vitruvian_hair.py`. Kills the #1 tell. |
| 2 | **Stabilize hair color across angles** | 2-3 h | Tint the anisotropic highlight and backlight from the hair albedo (not white/warm), cut hair-kicker energy ~3.2 → ~1.5, clamp specular to kill the blue sparkle (roughness floor / specular AA). |
| 3 | **Kill the skin speckle** | 2-4 h | The white glitter dots: lower/blur the SSS noise texture contribution, raise the skin roughness floor, check MSAA/alpha-to-coverage interaction on the overlay meshes. |
| 4 | **Fix the catch-light flood** | 1-2 h | Shrink the OmniLight range/energy or move it into a light cull mask that only hits the eyes. Removes the salmon neck *and* keeps the eye sparkle. Recheck the jawline seam after. |
| 5 | **Default look hygiene** | 1 h | Eyeshadow → 0 in `look_settings.json`; raise resting gaze to camera height; fix the slight convergence (cross-eye). |
| 6 | **Cinematic: no blink on the hero beat** | 1-2 h | Gate `_drive_face` blinks during 27-31s; while there, add `Pupil_Dilation` on the close-up push-in (it ships in L3). |
| 7 | **Mouth interior shading** | 2-4 h | Multiply mouth albedo toward black by depth into the bag (vertex paint or a cheap fresnel/AO term in the mouth material), scale tongue rest pose down ~15%. Muppet → mouth. |
| 8 | **Lighting + grade pass (biggest cheap win)** | 4-8 h | One authored "hero" preset: motivated key with softer wrap, cool rim, negative fill; background gradient + soft floor contact shadow instead of the grey void/horizon; WorldEnvironment glow-threshold, slight vignette, filmic tonemap tuning, subtle film grain. This is where "screenshot looks pro" lives. |
| 9 | **Eye contact AO + lash shadow** | 3-5 h | A thin upper-lid occlusion strip (dark gradient card or shader ring at lid line). Grounds the eyeball even before the real-eye swap. |
| 10 | **Shoulder spikes** | 2-4 h | Re-export the shirt with smoothed/normal-blown inflation (or inflate along smoothed normals) in `_mixamo_retarget.py`; the pointy seams are an artifact of raw-normal inflation. |

### Tier 2 — medium efforts (1-3 days each; this is the "credible" tier)

| # | Fix | Effort | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Real 4K skin textures** | 1-2 d | Bake `skin_light/dark_col` blend + `skin_cavity_rough_coat` + disp→normal from the EXRs into face textures (tile 1001) AND body tiles 1002-1004 (atlas-bake for Godot — no UDIM). Arms/hands/neck get real skin. Single biggest *material* upgrade. |
| 12 | **Real eyes** | 2-3 d | Export tile-1005 eyeballs with `sclera/iris` 4K maps, wire the 8 `Look_*` gaze morphs (lids follow gaze!), drop in `Tearline.blend` + `Lacrimal_Caruncle.blend`. Retire the procedural eyeball. Pairs with #9. |
| 13 | **Liveness layer** | 1-2 d | Breathing (chest bone scale + `Nostrils_Dilated` + shoulder rise), idle weight shift, head micro-sway (noise-driven), asymmetric resting face from the per-side L3 morphs, occasional `Thinking`/`Circumspect` flickers. Procedural, no new assets. Stillness is the deepest uncanny trigger after the eyes. |
| 14 | **Viseme lip-sync demo** | 1-2 d | The 21-viseme set + a small audio-amplitude or Rhubarb-style driver → the character *speaks a sentence* in the cinematic. This is the demo moment that makes people forward the video. |
| 15 | **Groom v2 from authored guides** | 2-3 d | Rebuild cards from `hairstyles/Bob.npz` or `Eve.npz` guide strands (authored flow/clumping) instead of procedural scatter; bake a proper flow/ID map into the atlas. Fixes lasagna crown + rope clumps at the root cause. |
| 16 | **Expression QA pass** | 1 d | Retune the curated 26: surprise should not bare teeth, sad needs brow+lip-corner amplitude, add `Smile_Lips_Closed` as default idle. Render a strip per expression and *look at it* (FACE_FORCE grid is already scripted). |
| 17 | **Wardrobe** | 1-2 d | Fabric normal/roughness on shirt+pants, simple shoes, fix the collar notch. Cheap realism multiplier in every full-body shot. |

### Tier 3 — needs different tech / accept the ceiling

- **Strand-based hair rendering** — Godot has no groom/strand system; compute-shader strands
  is a research project. Card hair has a hard ceiling; Tier-1/2 gets us to "good card hair"
  (think pre-2020 AAA), not MH Groom.
- **Dynamic wrinkle/tension maps** — possible in the skin shader (blendshape-driven normal
  blend) but it's a week+ of shader/baking work for a subtle gain at this fidelity level.
- **Scan-level micro-detail** (pore displacement, micro-spec): the source EXRs are good but not
  scan data; parity with MH's 3Lateral scans is not reachable from this asset, full stop.
- **GI/Lumen-class lighting** — Forward+ with SDFGI/SSIL can fake a lot; true MH-Creator-quality
  lighting parity is an engine-level gap. The Tier-1 grade pass matters more.

## 4. Honest utility verdict

**Can this beat MetaHuman on visuals? No — and it shouldn't try.** MetaHuman is built from
3Lateral facial scans with a groom system, strand hair, wrinkle maps, and a calibrated shading
model, backed by a nine-figure pipeline. After every fix in this document, Vitruvian lands
around "strong PS4/early-PS5-era realtime human" — genuinely good, not photoreal.

**What this actually is: the license-free digital human stack.** That position is real and
MetaHuman *cannot* occupy it:

| | MetaHuman | VitruvianGodot |
|---|---|---|
| License | Epic EULA — locked to Unreal Engine products, no redistribution of source assets | **CC0 character + MIT tooling** — use anywhere, ship anywhere, modify, resell |
| Engine | Unreal only | Godot (open source), assets are plain GLB — portable to anything |
| Footprint | Multi-GB, UE project required | ~170 MB self-contained exe; head GLB is 9.7 MB |
| Cloud/headless rendering | UE licensing + heavyweight | Godot headless renders on anything (already proven: the showcase mp4 is rendered via `--write-movie`) |
| Pipeline transparency | Black box (MH Creator service) | 100% scripted Blender→Godot, reproducible, hackable |
| Character variation | MH Creator (online service, EULA) | CharMorph presets (CC0) — ethnicity/body morphs scriptable offline |

Real markets where "CC0 + Godot + small" beats "gorgeous + EULA + Unreal": embedded/kiosk
avatars, privacy-sensitive and air-gapped deployments, research (redistributable stimuli!),
open-source projects that can't take the Epic EULA, indie/web (Godot export), agent/AI avatar
products where the vendor must own the full stack commercially, and procedural many-humans
generation.

**The bar the demo has to clear:** not "looks like MetaHuman" but "**a 15-second talking
close-up that doesn't trip the uncanny alarm**, from a 170 MB free-license exe". Today the
demo fails that in the first second (part-line, eyes, speckle). With **Tier 1 complete
(≈ 2-3 focused days)** the close-up stops being embarrassing; with **Tier 1 + items 11-14
(≈ 2 weeks)** — real skin, real eyes, breathing, and a *spoken sentence* — the pitch
"MetaHuman-class workflow, zero EULA, runs anywhere" becomes credible, because the viewer's
first reaction shifts from "what's wrong with her hair" to "wait, this is CC0?"

**Recommendation: continue, but reframe.** Stop measuring against MetaHuman renders; start
measuring against "the best digital human you can legally ship without anyone's permission."
Do Tier 1 before showing this to anyone else; do 11-14 before pitching it.
